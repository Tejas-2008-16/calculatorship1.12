The 3 Statutory Limits Under Section 10(13A) & Rule 2A

The Income Tax Department exempts HRA up to the minimum of the following three figures:

  1. Actual House Rent Allowance received from your employer.
  2. Actual rent paid minus 10% of your Basic Salary (+ DA).
  3. 50% of Basic Salary (+ DA) if living in a metro city (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai), or 40% for any other non-metro city.

Important Tax Filing Nuances

  • Landlord's PAN: If your annual rent paid exceeds ₹1,00,000 (approx. ₹8,333/month), submitting the landlord's PAN to your employer is mandatory under CBDT regulations.
  • Old vs New Regime: HRA exemption is not available if you opt for the New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC). Under the New Regime, the entire HRA received is added to taxable salary.
  • Paying Rent to Parents: You can legally claim HRA by paying rent to parents, provided the property is owned by them, rent receipts are maintained, and the parents declare that rental income in their respective income tax returns.
