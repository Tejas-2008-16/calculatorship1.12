The Exact CAGR Formula

CAGR measures the annual growth rate of an investment over a specified time span, assuming the profit is reinvested at the end of each year:

CAGR = ( (Final Value / Initial Value) ^ (1 / Years) ) - 1

CAGR vs XIRR: When to Use Which

  • CAGR: Ideal for one-time point-to-point investments (such as purchasing a share or property in 2020 and selling in 2026). It does not account for interim cash inflows or withdrawals.
  • XIRR (Extended Internal Rate of Return): Essential for monthly SIPs, recurring dividends, and SWPs where cash flows happen at different dates and each installment compounds for a different duration.
