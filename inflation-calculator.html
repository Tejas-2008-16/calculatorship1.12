The Mathematics of Future Value and Purchasing Power

Inflation acts as reverse compounding against idle cash. The two fundamental formulas used in this simulator are:

1. Future Value of an Expense (FV)

FV = Present Amount × (1 + Inflation Rate)^Years

2. Future Purchasing Power (Present Real Value)

Purchasing Power = Present Amount ÷ (1 + Inflation Rate)^Years
