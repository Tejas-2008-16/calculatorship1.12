GST Calculation Formulas: Inclusive vs Exclusive

The Goods and Services Tax (GST) operates under two common invoicing scenarios:

1. GST Exclusive (Adding GST to Base Amount)

GST Amount = (Original Cost × GST Rate) ÷ 100
Net Price = Original Cost + GST Amount

2. GST Inclusive (Extracting GST from Gross Retail Price)

GST Amount = Gross Price - [Gross Price ÷ (1 + (GST Rate ÷ 100))]
Pre-Tax Amount = Gross Price - GST Amount

Official GST Rate Presets & Classifications

  • 0% (Exempt): Fresh food grains, milk, salt, unbranded agricultural produce, and healthcare services.
  • 5%: Packaged food items, footwear below ₹1,000, rail freight, transport services, and essential medicines.
  • 12%: Processed agricultural products, business class air tickets, and specific construction equipment.
  • 18% (Standard Rate): Most software services, telecommunications, financial services, restaurants, and IT hardware.
  • 28% (Luxury & Demerit): Automobiles, pan masala, luxury goods, aerated beverages, and casino services.
