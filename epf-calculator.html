Understanding the EPF Contribution Split

Every month, a total contribution of 24% of your Basic Wage + DA is deposited into your Provident Fund accounts:

  • Employee Contribution (12%): 100% of this goes into your EPF account and earns statutory interest.
  • Employer Contribution (12%): Split into two distinct components:
    • 8.33% goes into the Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS), subject to a statutory wage ceiling of ₹15,000 (i.e. capped at ₹1,250 per month).
    • 3.67% (plus any excess above the EPS cap) goes into your EPF account and compounds with interest.

Tax Rules on EPF Contributions

Under Section 10(11) of the Income-tax Act, interest earned on annual employee contributions exceeding ₹2,50,000 (or ₹5,00,000 where the employer does not contribute) is taxable as income in the hands of the employee.
