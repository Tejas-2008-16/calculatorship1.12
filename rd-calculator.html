How Indian Banks Calculate RD Interest (Quarterly Compounding)

In India, commercial banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, etc.) and India Post calculate recurring deposit returns on a quarterly compounding basis. Each monthly installment earns interest for the exact number of remaining quarters in the deposit tenure:

Maturity Sum = Σ [ P × (1 + r / 4)^(4 × t_i) ]

Where t_i is the remaining fraction of the year for each installment i. This method gives a slightly higher yield than simple interest or annual compounding.

Taxation Rules for Recurring Deposits (FY 2026-27)

  • Taxable at Slab Rates: Unlike equity SIPs, interest earned on recurring deposits is fully taxable under "Income from Other Sources" according to your individual income tax slab.
  • TDS Threshold: Banks deduct 10% TDS under Section 194A if total interest earned across all your fixed and recurring deposits in that bank branch exceeds ₹40,000 in a financial year (₹50,000 for senior citizens).
  • Form 15G / 15H: If your total taxable income is below the basic exemption limit, you can submit Form 15G (or Form 15H for seniors) to prevent TDS deduction.
