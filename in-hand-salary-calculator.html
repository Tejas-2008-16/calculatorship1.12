Why ₹12 LPA CTC Never Equals ₹1 Lakh a Month

The Cost-to-Company (CTC) represents the total annual expenditure an employer incurs on an employee. A substantial portion of your CTC is withheld or diverted into mandatory statutory accounts before your salary slip is generated:

  • Employer EPF Contribution (12% of Basic): Bundled inside CTC, paid directly to EPFO.
  • Gratuity Provision (4.81% of Basic): Held as a statutory reserve under the Payment of Gratuity Act; accessible only after 5 years or separation.
  • Employee EPF Contribution (12% of Basic): Deducted from your monthly gross pay.
  • TDS (Income Tax): Deducted at source by your company accounts team every month.
  • Professional Tax: State-level levy of up to ₹200 to ₹300 per month.
