PFRDA Exit Rules and Annuity Mandates

The National Pension System (NPS), governed by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA), is a low-cost, government-backed retirement planning vehicle. Upon reaching the superannuation age of 60:

  • Lump-Sum Withdrawal: You can withdraw up to 60% of the accumulated corpus as a 100% tax-free lump sum under Section 10(12A).
  • Mandatory Annuity: You must purchase an annuity from an approved Life Insurance Service Provider with at least 40% of the corpus. The annuity provides a lifelong monthly pension.
  • Small Corpus Rule: If your total accumulated corpus is ₹5 Lakh or less, you are permitted to withdraw 100% of the corpus as a lump sum without purchasing an annuity.

Tax Deduction Privileges (Old vs New Regime)

NPS Tier-1 contributions offer unique triple-tax benefits under the Indian Income-tax Act:

  • Section 80CCD(1): Employee contribution up to 10% of salary (or 20% of gross income for self-employed), within the overall ₹1.5 Lakh ceiling of Section 80C.
  • Section 80CCD(1B): An exclusive additional deduction of ₹50,000 over and above the Section 80C ceiling (available under the Old Tax Regime).
  • Section 80CCD(2): Employer's contribution up to 14% of salary for Central Government employees (10% for private sector). Notably, this deduction remains available under both the Old and New Tax Regimes.
