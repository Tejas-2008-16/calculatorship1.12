The Simple Interest Formula

Simple interest is determined by multiplying the daily interest rate by the principal by the number of days that elapse between payments:

SI = (Principal × Rate × Time) ÷ 100
Total Amount = Principal + SI

Simple vs Compound Interest Comparison

Over long investment horizons, compound interest vastly outpaces simple interest because each interest payment is added to the principal to earn future interest:

  • ₹1,00,000 at 10% for 10 years with Simple Interest gives ₹2,00,000 total (₹1 Lakh interest).
  • ₹1,00,000 at 10% for 10 years with Compound Interest gives ₹2,59,374 total (₹1.59 Lakh interest).
